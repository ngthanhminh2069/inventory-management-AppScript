OpenWMS - Standard Operating Procedures & User Manual

Operational guidelines for FEFO lot tracking, in-transit purchase orders, inventory guardrails, and audit compliance.

SSOT Verified Version 1.0.0

1. Master SKU & Safety Stock Rules

Every tracked product must be registered in the Master SKU catalog with a unique ItemCode, Category, and Unit of Measure.

Reorder Threshold (MinStock):
  • MinStock > 0: Automatically triggers stockout warnings when usable on-hand stock falls below threshold.
  • MinStock = 0: Stockout alerting is bypassed (suitable for non-critical supplies).
  • Usable Stock vs Expired: Expired stock is segregated and excluded from reorder math to prevent phantom stockouts.

2. In-Transit Purchase Order (PO) Tracker

Provides pipeline visibility by tracking goods ordered from suppliers before physical dock arrival.

Effective Stock = Usable On-Hand + In-Transit Quantity
  • PO Life Cycle: OPEN → PARTIAL → RECEIVED.
  • Over-Receipt Guardrail: Inbound receipts cannot exceed the PO remaining balance. Excess attempts trigger hard stops.

3. FEFO Lot System & Expiry Badges

Implements First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) logic. Each batch is identified by ItemCode_LotNumber.

Non-perishable consumables without expiration dates are tagged as 'None' and never trigger expiry alerts.

4. Temporal Consistency Guardrail

Prevents chronological inconsistencies in warehouse accounting ledgers:

Rule of Physical Reality:

An outbound dispatch transaction (OUT) cannot be backdated to a calendar date earlier than the lot's earliest inbound arrival (IN).

T_dispatch(lot) ≥ min(T_inbound(lot))

5. Auditable Void Engine (Immutable Ledger)

To maintain financial and audit integrity, physical deletion of transaction rows is strictly prohibited. Mistakes are corrected exclusively via compensating reversal entries.

Reversal Entry

Voiding an IN generates a VOID_IN. Voiding an OUT generates a VOID_OUT. Original status changes to VOIDED.

Pre-Void Feasibility

Inbound transactions cannot be voided if remaining physical on-hand stock is lower than the void quantity (items were already consumed downstream).

PO State Rollback

If the voided inbound was linked to a PO, the received quantity is decremented and the PO transitions back from RECEIVED to PARTIAL or OPEN.
