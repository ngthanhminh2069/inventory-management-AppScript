OpenWMS v2.0 - Standard Operating Procedures & User Manual

Operational guidelines for FEFO lot tracking, lab bench in-use stock, accounts payable, landed fees, and audit compliance.

SSOT Verified Release v2.0

1. Master SKU & Storage Locations

Every tracked product is registered in the Master SKU catalog with unique ItemCode, Category, Unit, MinStock threshold, and physical Storage bin/rack.

Safety Stock & Phantom Prevention:
  • Usable Stock Only: Expired lots ($D \le 0$) are segregated and strictly excluded from safety stock evaluations.
  • Storage Sync: Inbound receipts automatically inherit the SKU default storage location if not explicitly overridden.

2. Multi-Line POs & Landed Obligations

Supports multi-item purchase orders and freight/handling fee tracking (FEE_LOGISTICS) to match supplier invoices.

Total PO Amount = Sum(Goods Lines) + Delivery + Handling + Other Fees
  • Pipeline Isolation: Fee lines are excluded from physical stock math ($I_{IT} = 0$).
  • Cancellation Safety: Cancelling a PO cancels all line items and fees simultaneously, provided no receipts or payments have occurred.

3. FEFO Lot System & Expiry Badges

Implements First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) picking. Each lot is tracked by composite key ItemCode_LotNumber.

Safe
> 30 Days
Near-Expiry
1 - 30 Days
Expired
≤ 0 Days

Expired lots are visually locked to prevent picking for customer shipments.

4. In-Use Bench Stock (Lab / Shop Floor)

Tracks opened containers and consumables in active use on workstations before complete disposal.

Bench Lifecycle:
  • Transition: Check "Mark In-Use on Bench" during Outbound to open a tracking card in IN_USE.
  • Depletion: When empty, click "Deplete" to close the bench lifecycle without affecting warehouse stock.
  • Void Guard: Outbound transactions whose bench items have been marked DEPLETED cannot be voided.

5. Accounts Payable & Supplier Payments

Records vendor invoices and payments directly tied to Purchase Orders with strict financial guardrails.

Anti-Overpayment Guardrail:
  • Cap: Payments cannot exceed the remaining unpaid balance of the PO ($A_{ ext{paid}} \le ext{RemainingPayable}$).
  • Status Transitions: UNPAID → PARTIAL → PAID.
  • Voiding: Voiding a payment decrements `PaidAmount` and rolls back PO payment status automatically.

6. Temporal & Over-Receipt Guardrails

Enforces physical reality and prevents inventory inflation at dock receiving:

  • Zero Over-Receipt: Received quantity cannot exceed the PO open balance ($ ext{QtyIn} \le ext{QtyOrdered} - ext{QtyReceived}$). Excess units trigger immediate hard stop.
  • Temporal Reality: Dispatch date cannot precede the earliest inbound arrival date of that specific lot.

7. Auditable Void Engine (Immutable Ledger)

Physical deletion of rows is strictly prohibited. All ledger corrections produce offsetting reversal records.

  • Reversal Entries: Voiding `IN` creates `VOID_IN`. Voiding `OUT` creates `VOID_OUT`. Original is flagged `VOIDED`.
  • Pre-Void Feasibility: Inbound void is rejected if physical on-hand is lower than void quantity (consumed downstream).
  • PO Reopening: Voiding an Inbound rolls back the PO received quantity ($ ext{RECEIVED} ightarrow ext{PARTIAL} ightarrow ext{OPEN}$).

8. Bulk Initial Inventory Import

Fast onboarding of existing warehouse stock via Excel upload or direct spreadsheet clipboard paste.

  • Excel / TSV: Download the pre-built template, fill in SKU, Lot, Expiry, and Qty, then upload or paste.
  • Batch Writing: Ingests hundreds of lots in 1-2 API calls, eliminating Google Apps Script timeout bottlenecks.
  • Zero Truncation: Codes with leading zeros (e.g. `'00123'`) are automatically preserved.
Full documentation, entity-relationship diagrams, and architectural blueprints are available in the local docs/ directory.
